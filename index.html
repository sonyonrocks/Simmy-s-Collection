Quick questions

What is the fabric like?

Cotton with a stretch. It is soft, breathable and easy to move in.

How do I pick my size?

The waist is elastic and stretches to fit, so sizes are by length: Small 36, Medium 37, Large 39 and Double Extra Large 40 inches. Use the Fit Assistant above for a quick suggestion.

What does delivery cost, and how long does it take?

Karachi: regular delivery is Rs 200 and takes 2 to 3 days. Same-day delivery in Karachi is Rs 300. Rest of Pakistan: Rs 250, and it takes 3 to 5 working days.

How can I pay?

Cash on delivery, or a transfer to our Easypaisa account. Card payments are not accepted.

What happens after I order?

You get a receipt on screen. Send your order to us on WhatsApp from the receipt page. We then call you within 12 to 24 hours to confirm your order and delivery.

What is the return and exchange policy?

You can return or exchange an item within 10 days. We do not give refunds. If an item is defective and you tell us within 7 days, it can be refunded.

Will the colour match the photo?

It will be very close, but lighting and screens can change how a colour looks. Message us on WhatsApp if you want to double-check a shade.
